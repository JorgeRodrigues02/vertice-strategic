Vértice Strategic Advisory is an independent boutique advisory firm specialised in structuring and intermediating business decisions between owners, investors and partners.

Most advisors step in once a business is ready. We enter earlier: when a project still lacks structure, when a business has stalled, or when an opportunity has no shape yet. We diagnose, structure and prepare — before any investor or buyer arrives.

Our role is to bring clarity, alignment and rigour to complex decisions, under clear mandates, with independence and transparent fee arrangements.

Services

What we do.

We engage selectively — only where we can add real value to a concrete decision.

01

Business Intermediation

SME sales and acquisitions, investor entry and exit, strategic partnerships and joint ventures. We prepare the parties, align expectations and support the negotiation.

02

Investment Readiness & Structuring

Projects with real potential that are not yet ready for capital: diagnosis, business plan, corporate and capital structure, and preparation for presentation to investors and funds.

03

Market Entry

Foreign companies entering Portugal and Portuguese companies expanding abroad, with a focus on the Portugal–Brazil–Angola corridor: partners, import/export, local representation and entry support.

04

Shareholder & Family Business Mediation

Deadlocks between shareholders, family succession and partners with diverging interests. An independent third party, mandated by all sides, to structure the decision — a third way between court and silence.

In every mandate: strategic diagnosis, risk mapping and a reasoned recommendation — go or no-go.

How we work

One mandate. Four phases. One decision.

01

Diagnosis

Reading the business, the parties and the context. Critical risks and real objectives.

02

Structuring

Transaction model, corporate structure, governance and financial principles.

03

Intermediation

Preparing the parties, aligning interests and strategic support in negotiation.

04

Decision

A reasoned final recommendation — go or no-go, against objective criteria.

Principles

  • We work only under formal mandates, with a retainer and a conditional success fee.
  • Confidentiality at every stage — a non-disclosure agreement before any sensitive information.
  • Each mandate brings in the right partners: lawyer, accountant, financial advisor.
  • If there is no real decision or alignment, we recommend not proceeding.

What we do not do

  • Operational execution or ongoing business management.
  • Accounting, auditing or legal services.
  • Grant, guarantee or manage financing or investments.
  • Guarantee outcomes or the completion of transactions.
